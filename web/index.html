pdf2page

Print a PDF two pages to a sheet, without the text shrinking to nothing.

Options

    Your files stay with you

    Everything happens in your browser, on your own computer. This site is only a handful of files that your browser downloads once; there is no server behind it for a PDF to be sent to.

    You can check: once this page has loaded, turn off your Wi-Fi and drop a PDF. It works the same.

    The page is also locked down so that your browser will refuse to let it contact any other website.

    What it does

    Law review articles and scanned book chapters often sit a small block of text in the middle of a big page. pdf2page measures where the ink is and trims the empty margins before placing pages side by side, so the text usually prints close to its original size.

    If trimming can't keep the text above 85% of its size, it says so instead: that file will read better printed normally.
